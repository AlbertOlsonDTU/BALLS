How it works. Drag on the field to aim from your launcher, release to fire your whole volley. Balls that reach the glowing gap in your friend's end cost them 1 HP each; the rest of their wall bounces balls back. Balls that come home land, and the first one home sets where you shoot from next. You gain a ball every turn, and new blocks grow in the middle every round. First to 0 HP loses.
