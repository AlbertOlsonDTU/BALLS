How it works. Drag on the field to aim from your launcher, release to fire your whole volley. Balls that reach the glowing gap in your friend's end cost them 1 HP each; the rest of their wall bounces balls back. Balls that come home land, and the first one home sets where you shoot from next. Each half of the field is your own Ballz board: before your turn, a new row is inserted at the centre line and pushes your half one row towards you. Blocks pushed past the red line into your end cost you HP. Special blocks: green frees an extra ball for your shot, orange bombs hit the 8 blocks around them, teal lasers clear their row, and pink ♥ heals you 2 HP. First to 0 HP loses.
